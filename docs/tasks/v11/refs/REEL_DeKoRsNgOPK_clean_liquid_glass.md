## Look (why it feels "clean")
- **Palette:** one hue family only — white #FFFFFF cards on pale periwinkle/sky bg (#D8E3FE→#EEF2FF), one brand blue (#2F6BFF-ish) for accents + hot words, near-black ink (#111) for main text. No second accent colour except a tiny red "46 new" badge.
- **Background:** soft blurred blue light blooms (big radial blobs, heavy blur) drifting at the top/edges of a white card; vertical light-streak "curtain" (blue bars blurred, like liquid glass refraction) behind laptops/mockups. Never busy — 70 % of every frame is empty white/air.
- **Glass:** frosted white panels with subtle 1px light border + large soft shadow; mockups are real product UI rendered small and crisp.
- **Text:** SOLID colours. Line 1 black, the subjective word(s) brand blue (e.g. "Right where your **team works**", "Book next to **your team**", "go to **waste**", "**500+ companies**", "launch **film**"). Big, tight tracking, heavy weight (~SF Pro Display Semibold/Bold). Text sits in the visual centre band, 1–2 lines, max ~5 words.

## Rules we take for P01–P07 v10
1. White/glass card on a pale bg + soft drifting blooms. Air > content.
2. Solid text: ink + ONE hot colour per video (rotate per scene only within that video's palette). Never gradient fill.
3. Every noun becomes a visual: people → face grid/avatars, replies → chat card + counter, time → clock roll, speed → countdown wheel, trust → count-up + logo marquee.
4. Transitions: blur-in/out, type-on with caret, input→card morph, circle wipe, whip blur, camera push into a 3D laptop. One technique per beat, ~1.5–2.5 s per beat.
5. Small real UI chips (pills, toggles, badges) as accents — not stock 3D objects.
6. End card: logo type-on + tagline with hot word + CTA chips + bloom glow.
