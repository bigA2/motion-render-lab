## Liquid glass (most wanted)
- **Glass pill over a wavy gradient**: rounded pill with a thin bright rim, a faint inner highlight at the top, the background slightly magnified + shifted inside the pill (refraction), and a coloured rim fringe (RGB split 1–2 px) while it moves. Our build: `backdrop-filter: blur(8px) saturate(160%)` + an SVG `feDisplacementMap` on a clone of the background for the lens bend, rim = 1px gradient border + inset shadow.
- **Bouncy glass shape morph**: one glass outline morphs pill → circle → wide bar (springy, ≤2% overshoot per our rule) over dark diagonal light streaks. Good as a transition/title device.
- **Glass blocks / cube**: translucent cubes in 3D that split apart and re-stack, lit by moving coloured light bars behind (blue). Use for "many apps → one workflow" moments.
- **Overlapping translucent petals → one glass icon**: coloured translucent shapes overlap (colour mixes where they overlap), spin, then collapse into one glossy rounded square (app icon). Then a fan of glass cards rotates in 3D and stacks. Use for logo reveal / "everything into one". Avoid an Apple-Photos-like flower; use our own shapes.

## AI prompt / chat (matches FansaChat exactly)
- **Prompt box types itself**; as the text wraps to 2 lines the camera pushes in and re-frames; a cursor arrives, the send button presses (scale 0.92) and pops.
- **Agent desktop flow**: device frame tilted → push in to "What should your agent do?" → user types a task → "Thinking…" shimmer → a plan card appears with a checklist (capabilities) → "Start building" button → pull back to the device.
